about

$ cat about_me.txt

Ninfixe Studio is an independent game studio founded in 2025 with a passion for creating atmospheric horror experiences. Every project is built with the goal of delivering not just scares, but memorable moments that stay with players long after they finish the game.

I believe horror is at its best when it tells a meaningful story. Instead of relying on random events or absurd twists, I enjoy crafting narratives that connect with players emotionally, making every character, environment, and decision feel like part of a larger experience.

Based in Lombok, Indonesia, I continue learning and building new projects with Unity and C#, always striving to create games that leave a lasting impression.
